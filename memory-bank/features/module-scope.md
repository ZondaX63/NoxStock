## Enterprise Scope To Add

The initial modules above are the current commercial foundation. The following capabilities are required before NoxStock can compete with mature market products:

### Inventory Operations

- Warehouses, branches, shelves, bins, and stock transfers
- Stock count, adjustment approval, reservation, and negative-stock policy
- Purchase/sales returns, batches/lots, expiry, serial numbers, warranty, and recalls
- Product variants, bundles, kits, unit conversions, multiple barcodes, images, and technical documents
- FIFO/weighted-average costing, stock valuation, reorder suggestions, dead stock, and ABC/XYZ analysis

### Accounting and Tax

- Chart of accounts, double-entry journals, periods, closing, opening balances, and reconciliation
- General ledger, trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, and cash-flow statement
- Withholding/tevkifat, exemptions, tax declarations, and Turkish e-Invoice/e-Archive/e-İrsaliye provider integrations
- Bank import/reconciliation, payment plans, installments, late fees, and exchange-difference postings

### Sales and Purchasing

- Purchase request/RFQ/supplier comparison and approval
- Partial receipt, backorder, delivery, shipment, cargo, and return workflows
- Price lists, customer groups, quantity pricing, campaigns, promotions, margin floors, and discount approval
- Customer credit risk and payment-term controls

### CRM and Platform

- Contacts, activities, tasks, opportunities, pipeline, segments, tags, and customer portal
- Global search, saved filters, bulk actions, import preview/rollback, exports, keyboard-first POS, and accessibility
- Versioned API, webhooks, payment/bank/cargo/marketplace/email/SMS integrations
- Two-factor authentication, separation of duties, KVKK workflows, backups, restore drills, observability, and load testing
