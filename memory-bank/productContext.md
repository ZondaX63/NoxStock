# Product Context

Last updated: 2026-07-10

## Why NexStock Exists

Small and medium-sized businesses often track stock, invoices, customer balances, supplier debt, cash accounts, and POS sales in disconnected tools. NexStock exists to make these operations visible and consistent in one web application.

## Problems It Solves

- Stock quantities are unreliable when purchases, sales, returns, and manual adjustments are not connected.
- Customer receivables and supplier payables are hard to follow without current-account history.
- Cash, bank, and credit-card balances drift when collections and payments are tracked separately from invoices.
- Owners need a fast daily dashboard instead of checking many screens.
- Personnel and permission management must fit real business roles.
- Retail sales need a fast POS flow alongside classic invoice workflows.

## Target Users

- Business owners
- Store managers
- Inventory staff
- Sales staff
- Accounting/finance staff
- Admin users managing company settings, users, and permissions

## Experience Goals

- Operational and fast, not marketing-like.
- Dense enough for repeated business use, but still readable.
- Turkish-first UX with clear business language.
- Workflows should guide the user through real commercial states: draft, sent, approved, paid, cancelled, completed.
- Dangerous operations such as approval, cancellation, stock correction, and payment reversal should be explicit and auditable.

## Product Principles

- Do not treat invoices, POS sales, stock, and finance as separate islands.
- Use business events and domain/application services to keep stock and money consistent.
- Prefer ABP conventions over custom infrastructure.
- Preserve auditability for every important commercial action.
- Add AI only after reliable business data and workflows exist.

## Long-Term Product Positioning

NexStock aims to become a Turkish-first, commercially trustworthy operating system for small and medium-sized businesses: fast enough for retail, disciplined enough for accounting, and broad enough to connect inventory, purchasing, sales, customer relationships, cash, bank, tax documents, and management reporting in one consistent ledger. “Best in market” means reliable business outcomes, not simply the largest number of screens: every feature must reduce manual work, prevent inconsistent records, explain its calculations, and remain usable in daily operations.
